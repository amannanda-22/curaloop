# 3-minute demo video: script

Record at 1080p with the app at `http://localhost:8000` (or the Vercel link). Voice in simple English, speak slowly.
Keep it under 3:00; the portal rejects nothing but judges stop watching. Do a dry run first and trim.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Worklist, Lakshmi pinned at top | "Lakshmi, 47, was screened at a camp in Madugula. She was referred to the CHC five days ago. Today the register says 'referred'. In most programmes that is where her story ends." |
| 0:20 | Click Lakshmi, show timeline and score breakdown | "CuraLoop keeps her as an open loop. She is on top because she is urgent, overdue, and one call has already failed. Every point of that score is visible." |
| 0:45 | Next-best-action card | "She said the bus fare is the problem. So the system does not just say 'try again'. It sends an ASHA with travel support." |
| 1:05 | Outreach, phone simulator: type the barrier reply in Telugu | "Her reply in Telugu is understood, the barrier is tagged, and anything that promises something waits for a human to approve." |
| 1:30 | Send a red-flag message ("bleeding") | "If she mentions a symptom, there is no clever answer. A fixed safety message goes out and a clinician is alerted." |
| 1:45 | Facility desk: confirm outcome | "She attends. Only now the facility, not the coordinator, can close the loop. That one rule is the whole point." |
| 2:05 | Insights: survival curve, process map | "For managers: how long it takes people to reach care, and exactly where they get stuck." |
| 2:20 | Fairness chart | "It also checks fairness. Far villages and women drop off more; the tool finds it so someone can act." |
| 2:35 | Impact tab | "How much does this help? We simulate it, we say the numbers are assumptions, and we show what is left if they are half right. The 90-day pilot in Anakapalle measures the real number." |
| 2:50 | Title card: CuraLoop, GitHub, link | "CuraLoop. Screened is not the same as cared for." |

Tips: use the Guided demo button for the clicks; hide bookmarks bar; close notifications; export at 1080p; upload to YouTube as Unlisted and test the link in a private window.
